Writing to us

These details make a reply faster:

  • Which app, and whether it is on iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple Watch.
  • The device's system version, from Settings under General, then About.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.

Please do not send your calendar, reminders, journal, notes or records unless we ask for a specific part. None of the apps has a Studio Spreza account, so we cannot look anything up on our side.

Kiwido

  • Kiwido needs Calendar. It shows your own events and reminders. It needs Calendar on every device, and Reminders is optional: without it, tasks you create stay on the Kiwido board. To give access later, open Settings, then Privacy & Security, and set Kiwido to Full Access under Calendars, and under Reminders if you want tasks in Apple Reminders. On a Mac, the same switches are in System Settings.
  • Where new items go. Tasks and events you create go to Apple Reminders and Apple Calendar, not to a separate Kiwido database, so they appear in Apple's apps too. If your device has no calendar or list Kiwido can write to, the item is kept on the Kiwido board instead of being lost, and the app tells you where it went.
  • Weather. Weather is off until you turn it on. On iPhone and iPad, swipe down from the top of the day to open the month, tap More (the ••• button), then Settings, then Weather. On a Mac, choose Settings from the Kiwido menu, then Weather. Turn on Show weather and pick a city, or let Kiwido use your location. Forecasts come from Apple Weather.
  • Alerts. Kiwido's alerts and morning summary are off until you turn them on in the app. If they stop arriving, check that notifications are allowed for Kiwido in Settings and that Background App Refresh is on, which Kiwido uses to keep upcoming alerts queued.

Your data

There is no Studio Spreza account and no Studio Spreza server holding what you enter. Kiwido keeps your events and reminders in Apple Calendar and Reminders. The other five apps keep what you enter on your device and, if sync is on, in your own iCloud.

  • Deleting an app removes its on-device data according to iOS behavior. Deleting Kiwido leaves your events and reminders in Apple's apps.
  • Removing synced data. For any app but Kiwido, you can remove the app's data from iCloud in Settings, under your Apple ID, then iCloud storage.
