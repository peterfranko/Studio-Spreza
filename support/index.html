Help for Kiwido, Upkeeper, Bountiful, Bilberry, Notation and Homegrown. If something here does not answer your question, email hey@studiospreza.com.

Writing to us

These details make a reply faster:

  • Which app, and whether it is on iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple Watch.
  • The device's system version, from Settings under General, then About.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.

Please do not send your calendar, reminders, journal, notes or records unless we ask for a specific part. None of the apps has a Studio Spreza account, so we cannot look anything up on our side.

Kiwido

  • Kiwido needs Calendar. It shows your own events and reminders. On iPhone and iPad it needs Calendar, and Reminders is optional: without it, tasks you create stay on the Kiwido board. On a Mac it waits until it has access to both. To give access later, open Settings, then Privacy & Security, and set Kiwido to Full Access under Calendars, and under Reminders if you want tasks in Apple Reminders. On a Mac, the same switches are in System Settings.
  • Where new items go. Tasks and events you create go to Apple Reminders and Apple Calendar, not to a separate Kiwido database, so they appear in Apple's apps too. If your device has no calendar or list Kiwido can write to, the item is kept on the Kiwido board instead of being lost, and the app tells you where it went.
  • Alerts. Kiwido's alerts and morning summary are off until you turn them on in the app. If they stop arriving, check that notifications are allowed for Kiwido in Settings and that Background App Refresh is on, which Kiwido uses to keep upcoming alerts queued.

Upkeeper

  • iCloud sync. Upkeeper syncs your records across your own devices through your private iCloud account. It is on by default, and you can turn it off in the app's Settings. If a change does not appear on another device, check that both are signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on.
  • A copy of your records. In Settings, under Storage, you can export a copy (a file Upkeeper can restore) or export for a spreadsheet (CSV).
  • Restoring a copy only adds. Items you are missing come back with their history, and existing items gain only logs they did not have. Nothing you already have is changed or removed, and restoring the same copy twice adds nothing. Restored items come back with nudges off, so turn on the ones you want.

Bountiful

  • iCloud sync. Bountiful syncs across your own devices through your private iCloud account. It is on by default, you can turn it off in the app's Settings, and Settings shows which mode it is running in under Storage.
  • A copy of your practices. In Settings, under Storage, you can export a copy (a file Bountiful can restore) or export for a spreadsheet (CSV).
  • Restoring a copy only adds. Practices you are missing come back with their history, and existing practices gain only entries they did not have. Nothing you already have is changed or removed. Restored practices come back with nudges off.

Bilberry

  • Place, weather and music. Bilberry asks for location the first time you tap Add Place, and for Apple Music the first time you tap Add Song. After that, entries you write that day pick them up on their own. To stop one, turn it off in the app's Settings under New Entries, or remove it from a single entry with a tap. If you said no to a permission and want it back, turn it on for Bilberry in the Settings app. The song comes from Apple's Music app on iPhone and iPad only.
  • iCloud sync. Bilberry syncs your journal through your private iCloud account whenever you are signed in to iCloud. There is no switch for it in the app; to stop it, turn off iCloud for Bilberry in Settings under your Apple ID.
  • Bringing in a journal. In Settings, under Import, you can bring in an export from Day One or Apple Journal, or Markdown files. Each import is listed there, and you can delete one whole import if it was not what you wanted; its entries go to Recently Deleted for 30 days. If an import doesn't look right, delete it from the same list and tell us at hey@studiospreza.com.
  • A copy of your journal. In Settings, under Export, you can save a full Bilberry Archive or Markdown files.
  • Require Face ID. Turn it on in Settings under Privacy. If Face ID or Touch ID does not work, your device passcode opens the journal.

Notation

  • Locked notes. A locked note opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. The lock is not a separate password, so there is nothing extra to forget: your passcode always opens it.
  • Hide Markdown. Turning it on in Settings, under Writing, hides the marks while you read and write. It changes only what you see; the text of your notes stays exactly as typed.
  • iCloud sync and Spotlight. Turn Sync with iCloud and Show notes in Spotlight on or off in the app's Settings. Locked notes never appear in Spotlight.
  • Bringing notes in, and taking them out. In Settings, under Import, you can bring in Markdown files, or notes exported from Bear, Obsidian or Notion. Each import is listed there; if one doesn't look right, delete it from that list and tell us at hey@studiospreza.com. Under Export, you can save every note as a folder of Markdown files or as a full Notation Archive. A single note can also be exported on its own.

Homegrown

  • Why it says "likely ready for a check". Homegrown never tells you to water on a timer. When a plant is likely ready, check the soil and answer Watered, Still damp or Not now. Still damp moves the next check out, and over time your answers teach Homegrown how quickly each plant dries in your home.
  • Going away. In Settings, under Going away, tell Homegrown when you will be gone. Nudges pause while you are away, and Homegrown can make a plant-sitter sheet to hand to whoever looks after your plants.
  • Local climate. Use local climate, in Settings under Your home, uses your location once to fit day length to where you live. It is optional, and Homegrown works without it.
  • iCloud and sharing. Homegrown syncs through your private iCloud account whenever you are signed in to iCloud; to stop it, turn off iCloud for Homegrown in Settings under your Apple ID. Anyone you invite to your household sees and can change every plant in it, and you can stop sharing at any time.
  • A copy of your plants. In Settings, under Export, you can save every record, your care and check-ins as spreadsheets, and your photos and videos.

Your data

There is no Studio Spreza account and no Studio Spreza server holding what you enter. Kiwido keeps your events and reminders in Apple Calendar and Reminders. The other five apps keep what you enter on your device and, if sync is on, in your own iCloud.

  • Deleting an app removes its on-device data according to iOS behavior. Deleting Kiwido leaves your events and reminders in Apple's apps.
  • Removing synced data. For any app but Kiwido, you can remove the app's data from iCloud in Settings, under your Apple ID, then iCloud storage.
